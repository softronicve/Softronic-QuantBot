CoinBuyPrice nowP&L Sell at stopSell at channelWhat the bot does

When it sells. On the stop: if the price touches the position's stop, which is set at the buy and does not move. On the exit channel: once a day, at 00:01:30 UTC, if the daily candle has closed below the low of the previous 10 candles; that level is recalculated every day. If neither happens, the position is held (HOLD). The bot does not sell for having made a given amount: it lets the rise run until the price turns.

Drawdown from peak

There is one point per daily cycle (00:01:30 UTC) and, with open positions, one more with the account as of now. BTC at the same exposure is the yardstick: what holding BTC would have returned with, each day, the same percentage of the account the bot had invested, without fees. If the account does not beat that line, picking coins and timing has added nothing over simply holding BTC.

Drawdown is how far the account is below its previous peak; max drawdown is the worst there has been.

Correlation between coins

These figures describe what already happened. With few trades they say nothing about the strategy: a figure only starts to count with dozens of closed trades. Correlation runs from −1 to +1: near +1, two coins rise and fall together, and holding both does not spread the risk.

What each figure means
Balance
The realized balance: the starting capital plus what was won or lost on trades already closed, minus fees. It does not change while a position stays open.
Account at current prices (equity)
The balance plus what the open positions are winning or losing right now. It is what would be left if everything were sold at this moment.
Notional value
The market value of the positions: units × price. "0.25x" means 25 % of the balance is invested. The limit is 1x: it never buys more than the account holds, so there is no leverage. Each coin is also capped at 10 % of the balance.
Portfolio heat (open risk)
What would be lost if all positions fell to their stop at once, in % of the balance. It is the planned worst case, not a current loss. If a new buy would push it above the limit, the buy is not made.
Margin used
The part of the balance locked as collateral for the positions. At leverage 1 it is their full value.
Entry channel · proximity · strength
The entry channel is the high of the previous 20 daily candles: it buys when a candle closes above it. Proximity is how far the close is from that high, and strength is how many ATR it closed above it.
ATR and stop
ATR is how much the coin moves on a normal day. The stop goes 2 ATR below the close it buys at, and the size is calculated so that hitting it costs about 1 % of the balance.
HOLD
Do nothing: with no position, wait for a breakout; with a position, keep it.
